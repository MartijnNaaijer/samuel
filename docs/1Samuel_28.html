Reading the scroll lines
  • ETCBC/DSS transcription, consonantal.
  • Black – letters preserved on the manuscript.
  • [ grey / in brackets ] – editorially reconstructed. In a lacuna running over three or more words only the first and the last word are shown; the words between them are left blank but keep their width, so the size of the gap stays visible.
  • א̇ (dotted underline) – an identified but uncertain reading.
  • ° – a letter present only as illegible traces.
  • . – a lost letter inside a lacuna.
  • The verse-final ׃ is not printed in the scroll lines.
  • Each verse shows the MT (pointed BHSA text) on top and the scroll line(s) beneath it, keyed by siglum, for direct comparison.
  • Hover over any word to see its lexeme (in ETCBC transliteration), part of speech, verbal stem & tense (for verbs), person, gender and number.
